C-5.7 now says: **"Automatic resolution is exactly as safe as
`kill --confirm-dead`: the census is the same, no weaker and no stricter.
Verified-empty quarantines release within one pace, including on the same boot;
no reboot is required."** Its documented residual is an orphan that leaves the
process group, starts a new session, scrubs both `SUBFLEET_ATTEMPT` and
`SUBFLEET_ROOT`, and outlives its recorded parent. Both resolution paths can
release while it lives. Ordinary detached children retain their markers and
hold the quarantine. The independent retention `lsof` check still protects
held writable files, directories and mappings; read-only file descriptors are
not retention holds. Saved boot observations remain diagnostic and never pin
an otherwise verified-empty census. Missing launch child publication remains
a conservative hold when a guardian's start receipt has no child or exit
receipt; a proven reboot can discharge that particular old launch.
