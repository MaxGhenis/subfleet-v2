Replacement PR description (remote PR not edited)

Canonical resource identity prevents differently spelled native UUIDs and
filesystem aliases from taking independent writer leases. UUID subjects use
their canonical parsed spelling; insensitive filesystem comparisons use
NFD(casefold(NFD(path))), default-ignorable removal and conservative dotless-i
equivalence. Display spellings remain intact. Legacy lease aliases remain
guards, reservation rechecks ownership, and the oldest legacy output owner
publishes while superseded owners settle and release their leases.

This round confines the live-output census to submit, preserves held output
leases during export-only identity failures, refuses exact quarantine
duplicates, permits reopening existing conversation bindings, covers native
attempt holders, and refuses requested output loops with exit 7. Rolling back
to 2.1.11 requires draining jobs or the documented atomic spelling repair;
otherwise exports may be silently skipped and revive guards retained. True
hung-filesystem deadlines remain documented with a strict xfail.
