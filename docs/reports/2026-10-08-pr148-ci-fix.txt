D-2 proof and remaining limit

The e2e file, STOP_DUMP_MARGIN_S, watch_stop and stop_request are unchanged from
release (the two functions' AST source segments are byte-identical). In each
checkout, a foreground-owned Python 3.12 child called the actual watch_stop with
a 5-second grace. Normal dump setup exits 1. Wrapping dump_traceback_later with a
3.3-second delay before calling the real function makes the independent kernel
timer fire at 8 seconds, before the dump's now-delayed deadline at 8.3 seconds.

checkout | normal exit / recorded PID | delayed exit / recorded PID
release/217 | 1 / 78853 | -14 / 83587
fixed head | 1 / 87874 | -14 / 91801

Every child was waited for and reaped. This proves the same timing-dependent
exit-status mechanism on the release branch; it does not claim to reproduce
daemon adoption end to end in this sandbox. The unchanged release unit test
test_c5_8a_a_blocked_faulthandler_dump_cannot_keep_the_process_alive also passed
and explicitly checks -SIGALRM. The watchdog documents that a slow or blocked
dump may be cut short by SIGALRM. Moving/delaying that fallback would weaken
the existing stop bound; reliably guaranteeing exit 1 requires a broader change.
D-2 is reported without weakening its test or production backstop.
