## C-8.4 changes and their reasons

The source-admission wording explicitly includes reservation as well as preparation; #140 does not independently fence that source, while #142 does.

The full clauses below are the reviewed before text and final after text for each head. Reasons for every semantic change:

1. Add #139's early Git/cwd fence look on #140 and preserve it on #142. An eligible turn under a fence runs no workspace git, while the transaction still catches a later fence.
2. State that every reservation fences its resolved run folder and every detached job holds a reader row there throughout its job lease, including retries/quarantine. Record and use the reservation's resolved folder at launch, preventing a later retarget of the submitted symlink from moving the provider away from its pin. This adds retention protection without changing C-6.5's writer contention.
3. Broaden quarantine cleanup's row description to detached readers/writers, and remove the detached-writer quarantine-location limit: the new reader row is visible to `_fence_quarantine` on that location, including when the attempt is quarantined and the job is lost. A new regression exercises actual archive/recovery/reclamation there.
4. On #140 remove the detached-reader admission limit, because its resolved run-folder fence is now checked in the reservation transaction. Keep the allocated writer's source-admission limit: #140 fences its allocated cwd and target, while source preparation and reservation still lack #142's source-folder checks.
5. On #142 remove that remaining source limit and describe early recorded/current source-folder checks, folded legacy admission, retention diagnostics, seen fences and priority preservation. Recheck the prepared resolved reader/in-place cwd that is actually pinned.
6. Qualify fully spellable new submissions by whether their canonical cwd matches the owning allocation's recorded root. Add queued fresh canonical pin misses against raw aliased allocation roots: exact-root MIXEDCASEPARENT (`=` compares case), and descendants under STÄTE recorded as stäte (`LIKE` folds ASCII only). The trees survive through ownership-boundary error/rollback, not `worktree-in-use`. The bulk SQL pin joins use stored paths on both sides, and also run while committing resumed journals. Closing this gap would require a canonical allocation-root census carried into those call sites; filesystem canonicalization inside that transaction is forbidden. This round keeps the gap documented and does not merge #141's separate case-alias work.
7. Retain the remaining unspellable/quarantine-time, exact-root and legacy Unicode/case pin qualifications. On #142 retain the distinction between recorded pin strings and precomputed folded legacy admission strings. Preserve all existing archive, process-holder, job-directory, salvage, rollback and pacing clauses.
