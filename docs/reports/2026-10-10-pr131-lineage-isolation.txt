PR #131: isolate child-run lineage from parent turns
Base: e331a3edd0665a1423098a54b109d1aa2ee443c0
Branch: feat/quarantine-self-resolve, local metadata in .git-local

Cause (line numbers at the base commit):
- daemon.py:6354 passes the shared workdir to procs.containment without other
  live attempts' ownership. procs.py:769-772 treats a cwd match as a root unless
  its different attempt marker was visible and verified. Root-only markers
  take the same path through procs.py:763-766.
- procs.py:782 captures the process identity AND its group. daemon.py:6367-6373
  persists these roots with attempt.lineage_recorded. procs.py:642-645 expands
  the retained group on subsequent censuses. Adopting the parent's waiter
  therefore adopts its guardian, provider and remaining group members too.
- This is census discovery, not parent_job_id lookup or an upward ancestry
  walk from the submitting process. Host event 20 recorded parent ownership
  before child lineage events 25/26, but the census never consulted that record.
  Stored evidence omits commands/environments, so it cannot distinguish the
  first positive cwd read from a root-only marker read; both are reproduced.

Fix:
- Read other live attempts' launch receipts, owned identity histories and
  provider identities. Require verified identities to anchor foreign groups.
- Stop descendant walks at another attempt's boundary, protecting a nested
  child's verified launch. Exclude foreign roots from every census source and
  from process/provider ownership recording, including fresh cwd/marker reads.
- Remove previously adopted foreign evidence. Carry transient exclusions for
  group members not yet recorded by their owner's paced inspection.
- An absent foreign leader cannot establish ownership of a potentially reused
  group. Census roots still grant no signal authority; owned survivors hold
  leases, and unknown inspection results remain conservative.
