## C-8.4 reasoning

Release's job-directory readers, canonical actual cwd, nested `worktree-lease`
protection, quarantine reader protection and updated retention scheduling text are
all retained. #139 adds the early preparation guard for both the Git folder and
actual cwd. Its guarantee concerns an eligible turn whose early look finds the fence still
held in its state-rechecking transaction. Earlier conversation or pin holds keep
their semantics; later placement checks and refusals follow the fence hold. A
cancelled or ended turn receives neither that hold nor workspace preparation,
and a fence acquired later is still caught by the reserving transaction.

Neither #139 nor #144 fixes the remaining C-8.4 limits. Keep detached writers'
exact-folder reservation check, exact-root/legacy case aliases, quarantine-time
case spelling, and detached writers whose leases name quarantine locations.
The distinction between an in-place workspace and its removable job directory is
also retained. The full before and after clauses follow.
