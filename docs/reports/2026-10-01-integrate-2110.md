Legacy views remain reachable as separate cards authenticated by immutable approval ID. Unmatched event cards cannot submit drafts. A replacement-before-events regression covers identical questions; existing exact-ID and identical-tool tests cover tool cards.
3. The pinned Review action reveals an inline question form and opens a tool request sheet only for tools. The sheet decision is a tested function used by the UI, not a test-only simulation.

The final decision rereads a persisted Stop under both locks, uses the already-gathered evidence once, and retains delivery-unknown blocking when delivery cannot be established. A recorded Stop with established or ruled-out delivery cannot produce a later unfinished-turn block.
5. The UI queue follows the daemon's full order: repair messages, missed steers, then ordinary messages, retaining sequence order within each class.
6. #65 is already in the candidate's ancestry at `52ffde17`; its ultracode default and unlimited question wait survive. Titles and steer preserve each conversation's settings. Current `_workspace` returns three values and inspected mocks match its arity. #72 admission keeps conversation turns in their separate attended pass and pool; titles do not consume a new admission.
7. The stricter card gate initially prevented a newly arriving question from mounting its form before identification. Every pending card now runs the safe approval lookup, including questions; a failed initial question lookup exposes Reload request. Modern views enable the existing exact row, while legacy views create a separate row and fresh draft. Independent review verified both flows and the retry path.
8. C-29.13 explicitly includes approval cards and sheets. Their fixed font overrides bypassed the readable-text settings. UIApprovals now uses the established readingFont styles and inherited reading body text, preserving the persisted scale.
9. App metadata remains 2.1.10/build 15. No installation, launch, push, or running-daemon change is part of this integration.
