The app now uses neutral surfaces, a centered reading column, one-line sidebar rows, and a unified composer. Interim agent messages carry live progress, with tools grouped between messages; finished tool history collapses under its duration and step count. Approval scope is listed before commands and bulk content in a bounded scroll area, with decision buttons kept visible. Question cards show the answer picker first and show each question and its submitted answer once in settled history.

Intentional differences from the reference apps: Subfleet keeps SF Pro and the Mac accent color, shows routing and account usage, and retains its Continue and Leave recovery actions. Every finished turn shows “Completed” (or its settled failure, stopped, or withdrawn outcome) and a serving line naming the account, model, and effort, including on text-only turns. The header also shows current account usage. Raw request JSON stays under Details; named protocol metadata and nulls are omitted from the grant summary, while unknown fields remain visible. Provider marks are simplified monochrome vectors.
