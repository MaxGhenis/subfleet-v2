# Operator holds say what they are

D-RAMP-HOLD-LABEL, 2026-10-26. Base: release/217, `4a40fbe45534`.

The ramp holds lanes to a six-hour safety bound, refreshes those holds, and releases lanes in rotation within minutes. Status instead called an operator hold an account closure with a reset time. Two sessions treated that bound as hours of unavailable capacity; one moved a review to the app login and another left work waiting.

## Decisions as built

- `subfleet lanes hold <lane> --until T [--note TEXT]` accepts at most 120 printable characters on one line. Both CLI and daemon refuse overlong text, line breaks and control/nonprintable characters before any write. Empty text becomes no note; other printable text is preserved exactly, including Unicode and spaces.
- Schema migration 10 adds nullable `closures.note`; the `Closure` domain record has an optional trailing `note`. `source_event` could not be reused: the daemon already records `operator`, the store persists that provenance, rendering prints it, and re-enrollment copies it. The importer records `v1-cooldown` for its separate limit rows and does not overwrite operator holds. Provider closures store no note.
- C-9.6's existing merge rule remains: equal/earlier ends refresh the unchanged row and retain its note; a later end replaces the row's metadata, taking the new call's note or NULL when omitted. A new hold after release takes only its own note. Status/probe refreshes, independent readers, re-enrollment and release retain the recorded note. This makes explicit the distinction between refresh and replacement; no admission clock was changed.
- Operator hold text is `<home>: held by the operator: <note> (until <T> at the latest)`, or `<home>: held by the operator (until <T> at the latest)` without a note. A context without a home uses its lane identifier. The audit covered alerts and delivered notices, status's closure section, capacity rendering, lane listings, the hold acknowledgement, routing explanations, pinned-job notices/failures and run-show notices, morning briefs, and conversation wait summaries. Hold alerts use distinct keys so a simultaneous provider alert cannot hide them.
- Closure JSON retains `reason` and `until_at` and adds `note`, null when absent. Offline reads of old stores report null without migrating them; error JSON includes the note too. The fixed status-file schema carries hold text through its existing alerts.
- Notes remain explanatory. The scheduler copies closure evidence into explanations, but its admission filters, clocks, scopes and ordering do not inspect notes. The property compares the full admission result and evidence after removing only the explanatory `note` field, across live, expired, released, account and model holds.
- Provider limits, credits, cooldown and `auth-dead` keep their wording and never display a note. The hold and a provider closure keep their separate explanations even when their clocks match.
- C-9.6 and its change log were updated with the code. Nothing under `~/reviews` was edited; the ramp's later `--note` change remains the hub's work.
