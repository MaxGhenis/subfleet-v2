C-5.1, C-5.5, C-5.12 (2026-10-16, PR #220 fix rounds 2–4): a responsibility root without a recorded unique id needs a matching live snapshot and one fresh start-time read before the member scan. After every applicable scan, for either kind of root, one in-process kernel read checks the current holder. A readable successor unique id excludes only members born at or after that successor; real members observed before root death are retained. An absent or unreadable holder, or a successor without readable ordering, preserves collected provisional evidence with an unverifiable result. A known-id root already gone with no provisional members can still verify empty. A changed start on a root without a recorded unique id also reports the identity diagnostic. Member live state and birth identities come from the same kernel records as their unique ids, so an older snapshot holder or a later pid holder cannot replace that observation. Different ownership and responsibility births at one pid remain alternative census seeds; pruning requires every recorded birth to be provably different. Same-second reuse and a root dead before a member read remain C-5.3/C-5.5 limits; missing successor ordering may conservatively retain strangers.
