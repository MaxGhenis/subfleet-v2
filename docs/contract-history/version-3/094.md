Review of the descriptor hotfix (2026-09-25): C-16.1's cap counts only connections still read. `wait`s whose clients had left had held every place, so every client could be refused. The daemon now drops what a departed client sent that no pool has started, answers busy when no reader thread can start, and counts and reports busy answers in `daemon.status`. It listens with the system's largest queue, and C-16.1 says a failed `accept` loses its connection. C-15.4 counts a `wait`'s deadline from when the daemon read it. The CLI's wait loops and the gate poll ask again after a busy answer, and `daemon status` reports a busy daemon as running. C-29.2: the app shows a busy daemon as busy, keeps a send answered busy queued, and checks availability again once the feed answers.
