C-8.4, C-13.4, C-17.1 (2026-09-29, Max's d635 ruling; design `docs/desktop/retention-archive.md` revision 2): retention archives before it deletes. Everything of a finished job that exists nowhere else (every file of its allocated worktree and job directory, the worktree's admin directory and every object it names, its salvage commits, its rows) goes into an archive retention owns and is read back; only then are the rows deleted, and only entries whose signature is unchanged right before their unlink are deleted, anything else going to `retention-conflicts/`. A salvage ref in the verified archive bundle is referenced elsewhere. A pass retires a bounded batch oldest first without sizing everything first, parks a slow job with its progress and defers busy ones. New verbs `retention archives | restore | survey`.
