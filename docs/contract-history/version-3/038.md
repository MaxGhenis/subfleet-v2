A job waiting for a lease holds no later job back (2026-10-06, review of fenced-turn-precheck P3-4, measured on 0812d5c5): C-6.9, C-26.9, with a pool cap set (`conversations.max_active_turns`, `caps.max_active_attempts`, or any count that makes `scheduler.hold_scope` "pool"), a job held `lease-held` was a C-6.9 waiter like one waiting for a slot, so every later job of its tier that competed with it was held `behind-older-job` for as long as the lease was held, with slots free: a turn on retention's fence for the whole retirement, a turn on its conversation or native session for a whole turn. C-6.9's FIFO exists so a later job cannot take the slot an older one waits for, and a job waiting for a lease waits for none; that hold dates from when every unplaced job was a waiter (034d3d31), before C-26.9's lease queue, and the uncapped default had already dropped it (1b38d641, PR #72). Now a waiter on a lease, one held by another or kept for an older job, on this pass or on its clock, holds no later job back (`ahead`); in a pool with a fleet count a passing job keeps the last slot for it, as for any older waiter, and its lease keeps its order through the queue. A lease kept for an older job (`queued`) now counts among what such a job waits for, so it is not taken for a slot waiter either.
