C-9.10, after the review of #132 (2026-10-05, its five P3 follow-ups): an `ok` read whose cards block is missing or ineligible no longer erases the cards the last listing read saw, so an unused card stays warned on; it is recorded beside them as `cards_unlisted`, the kept cards are judged lost from the read that last listed them, and their alerts say so. A login folder that answers as another account drops what it kept of the old one when the profile names the new one, so a failed claim-status read can no longer date the new account's cloud credit from the old claim. The heal turn's output is read as UTF-8 with bad bytes replaced, and its process group is killed however the turn ends. `subfleet cards --help` no longer says "read only": `--refresh` may spend a heal turn. `lapsed` stays "`claude_free` or `canceled`", either on its own, now with its grounds.
