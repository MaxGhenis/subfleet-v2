PR #43 on the desktop line (2026-09-27): the line's descriptor hotfix (C-16.1 as it stood, 0100da6 and its reviews) gives way to #43's C-16.6 and C-16.7, and C-16.1 is the wire format again. The limit is raised to 65536, with fallbacks, and launchd's plist sets it too; the cap follows the limit; each connection has a reader thread of its own; reads have a 60 s idle close; a departed client's reads are dropped but its writes run (the line had dropped every request no pool had started); and `Client.call` asks a busy daemon again, so the gate's poll and the re-send of a lost `submit` or `kill` rely on it alone; the long-poll loops (`wait`, `kill --wait`, `run --wait`) keep their own retry. Kept from the line: C-16.5's lookups pool, the busy answer when no reader can start, and the exit after 300 s of failed `accept`s. New: the reserve grows by two descriptors per turn runner going (its relay connection, C-26.4); the conversation reads join C-16.7's read-only requests; `conversation.events` and `conversation.watch` end when their client leaves and, like `wait`, have a thread for every connection. `daemon.status` reports `descriptors`, which replaces the line's `connections`.
