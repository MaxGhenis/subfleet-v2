Review of 39223c9, finding 4 (2026-09-26): C-5.1 now says how a guardian's launch gate works and that it sits above descriptor 2, as C-30.1's fence does. The daemon took each gate (an attempt's, a probe's, a Claude lane re-enrolment's) from `os.pipe()`, which returns the lowest free descriptors: in a daemon whose fd 0, 1 or 2 had been closed after it started, the read end took that number, the guardian's /dev/null standard stream replaced it, and the guardian read end-of-file after the daemon had written the byte and exited 127 without starting the provider. The gates and the fence now come from one allocator, `procs.pipe_above_stdio`.
