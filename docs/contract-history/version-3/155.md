C-9.10, after the reviews of #133 (2026-10-05): a login whose only card ended unused no longer reads "reset card ended unused (…); no reset card": "no reset card" is said only when the cards block lists none. The `card-lost` and `credit-lost` lines of `status` and `subfleet cards` name the card or credit; they had read `grant`, which those warnings do not carry, and named nothing. A card or credit recorded at a lapse whose end, as last seen, had already passed is recorded `expired`, not `lapse`, so its alert no longer says it was lost with its plan (a card listed unused that ended while the reads between listed no cards, or failed, and then the plan lapsed).
