Events, each pool in a pass's result, and log lines report the bytes freed apart from the bytes moved into the archive. A job's salvage commits are parents of its anchor, the bundle's only head, so a salvage commit a network remote already holds no longer wedges its job. A job whose size cannot be measured is decided, not waiting, and the daemon's catch-up backs off while passes change nothing; an error keeps the archive cache and backs off; a slice always moves forward, and parked jobs take turns. The conversation service rebuilds a worktree without a repository-wide prune. `retention survey --sample N` estimates what retirement frees.
