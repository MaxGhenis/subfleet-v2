C-5.8a: a stop normally dumps up to 100 Python thread stacks and exits 1 after 30 s; an independent kernel SIGALRM deadline at 33 s bounds nested signals, faulthandler failure or cancellation, and slow or blocked dumps without needing the GIL or a new thread. Reentrant arms cannot replace the timers. launchd and `subfleet daemon stop` each provide a 40 s SIGKILL backstop only for stops they initiate (2026-09-25 incident; 2026-09-27 round-4 review; `docs/reports/2026-09-25-daemon-stop-wedge.md`).
