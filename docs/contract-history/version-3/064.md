C-26.9 and C-6.9 (2026-09-27, Max): the two turn caps default to null, which is no cap. With neither cap set, a turn waits only when no lane can take it (a closure, a disabled lane, the desktop login, a probe it would need) or a lease it needs is held (its conversation's, its native session's, or a writable checkout's), never for a count of other turns and never `behind-older-job` behind another turn, because an uncapped pool has no slot a later turn could take from an older one. A policy may still set either cap to a positive whole number, and then the turn pool keeps its FIFO as before. Turns also keep FIFO on a lease, capped or not: a turn held waiting for a lease (a writable checkout, a conversation, a native session) is queued for it, and no later turn of the pass takes it, even when the holder released it after the pass began; the later turn is held `lease-held` with the free lease under `queued` (not `leases`, which lists only leases another job holds) and `queued_behind` naming the older one, and `why` says the lease is kept for an older turn (reviews of 1b38d641 and 8f14a50f: a lease freed mid-pass went to whichever turn looked next). Between two turns only a writable checkout can in practice be contested: one conversation has one live turn and its own native session. Detached jobs cannot share an output path or a writable checkout (submit refuses the second), and two detached resumes of one session are pinned to one lane and model, so C-6.9 already orders them: the queue is the turn pool's. Incident: a conversation's turn waited 12 minutes with "Waiting for capacity" because the only two open Claude lanes each ran another conversation's turn under the old default of one turn per lane (three across the fleet).
